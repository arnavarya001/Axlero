## 📐 Consolidated 3-Page Architecture (Figma Starter Tier Compliance)

Due to Figma Starter plan limits (max 3 pages), the 7 mandatory PRD deliverables are consolidated into:

1. **`01_Discovery & Wireframes`**  
   - `01_Cover` (Thumbnail, team roles, status: Mid Review Ready)  
   - `02_Research & Flows` (Shopper & Vendor Personas, IA Tree)  
   - `03_Wireframes` (Grayscale Home, Search, PDP, Cart)
2. **`02_Design System & UI Kit`**  
   - `04_Design System` (Tokens, Color Variables, Typography scales)  
   - Interactive component matrix (Buttons, Inputs, Cards with Auto-Layout)
3. **`03_HiFi & Handoff`**  
   - `05_High-Fidelity UI` (Pixel-perfect Consumer & Vendor screens)  
   - `06_Prototype` (Interactive Smart Animate flows)  
   - `07_Developer Handoff` (Redlines, token specs, 8pt spacing guides)

- **Location:** Jaipur, Rajasthan

- **Primary Device:** Android Mobile (`360 × 800`) & Tablet Viewport

---

## 🎯 Core Goals & Motivations
1. **Effortless Product Cataloging:** Wants to upload new handcrafted catalog items directly from his mobile phone with quick photo capture and variant pricing (Size, Color).
2. **At-a-Glance Revenue & Order Metrics:** Needs clear, uncluttered daily analytics: Total Revenue, Pending Orders, Low Stock Alerts, and Payout Balances.
3. **Brand Trust & Verified Identity:** Wants his store profile to look professional, displaying his certification, customer reviews, and artisan story to build buyer confidence.
4. **Quick Dispatch Workflow:** Straightforward order fulfillment: print packing slips, update shipping tracking numbers, and view customer shipping addresses in 2 taps.

---

## ⚠️ Pain Points & Frustrations
- **Bloated Enterprise Portals:** Desktop-only seller portals with nested menus that are impossible to navigate on a smartphone while managing workshop operations.
- **Complex Inventory Forms:** Cluttered 20-field product listing forms that crash or lose draft progress if connectivity fluctuates.
- **Delayed Order Visibility:** Notification lags that cause delayed dispatches and lower merchant ratings.
- **Opaque Commission Deductions:** Marketplace fees that are not clearly itemized per transaction.

---

## 📱 How He Uses ShopVerse Vendor Dashboard
- Opens the **Vendor Dashboard** tab to monitor today's sales KPI cards (`$3,420 Revenue`, `18 Orders pending dispatch`).
- Uses the **4-Step "Add Product" Flow** (Basic Info ➔ Media Upload ➔ Variants & Pricing ➔ Stock & Review) to list new seasonal products in under 3 minutes.
- Monitors stock health via automated Low Stock indicators before units hit zero.

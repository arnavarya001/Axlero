# User Persona: The Shopper (Priya Sharma)

> **Persona Role:** Primary Consumer Archetype for Multi-Vendor E-commerce Experience

---

## 👤 Demographics & Profile
- **Name:** Priya Sharma
- **Age:** 27
- **Occupation:** Senior Marketing Associate (Remote/Hybrid)
- **Location:** Bengaluru, Karnataka
- **Tech Comfort:** High (Uses iPhone 14, UPI, Instagram shop tags, rapid delivery apps)
- **Primary Device:** iOS Mobile App (`390 × 844`)

---

## 🎯 Core Goals & Motivations
1. **Discover Unique Independent Sellers:** Wants curated, high-quality artisanal home decor, apparel, and lifestyle goods without dealing with unvetted spammy sellers.
2. **Unified Multi-Vendor Cart:** Wants to buy items from multiple boutique vendors in a single checkout session without paying separate exorbitant delivery charges or tracking 5 different tracking numbers manually.
3. **Frictionless, Rapid Checkout:** Relies on mobile payment methods (UPI, Apple Pay) and expects checkout to complete in 3 screens or fewer during busy work breaks.
4. **Order Transparency:** Clear estimated delivery dates per vendor and easy real-time order status tracking.

---

## ⚠️ Pain Points & Frustrations
- **Cognitive Overload in Checkout:** Traditional marketplaces redirect across 5–7 confusing screens (Address ➔ Delivery Slot ➔ Vendor Review ➔ Coupon ➔ Payment Gateway ➔ OTP ➔ Confirmation).
- **Hidden Fees & Inconsistent Shipping:** Surprise shipping fees added at the very last confirmation screen.
- **Detached Merchant Identity:** Can't tell whether a seller is reputable, certified, or local.
- **Cluttered Visual Interfaces:** Overcrowded screens with aggressive flashing banners, intrusive popups, and tiny unreadable tap targets.

---

## 📱 How She Uses ShopVerse
- Opens the app via curated notifications or search for specific sustainable brands.
- Uses horizontal filter chips (`Price: Low to High`, `Rating 4.0+`, `Fast Delivery`).
- Checks the **Vendor Spotlight** and verified seller badge (`★ 4.8`) before adding to cart.
- Adds items from 2 different vendors into one cart and completes the 3-screen checkout in under 90 seconds.

---

## 💬 Representative Quote
> *"I want to support genuine local brands, but I don't have the patience for cluttered interfaces or checkout flows that feel like filling out a tax form on my phone."*
